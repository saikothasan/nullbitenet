## Installation Guide

1. Purchase and download the Travel Adventure Blog template files
2. Access your Blogger dashboard
3. Go to Theme > Edit HTML
4. Create a backup of your current theme
5. Upload the Travel Adventure Blog template files
6. Customize the template to match your travel brand and style
7. Add your travel stories, photos, and tips
8. Save and publish your exciting new travel blog
